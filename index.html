07 Custom simulation & decision-support tools Turn technical models and data into tools that design teams, sales teams and decision-makers can actually use. Product selectors · Cost–benefit calculators · Simulation interfaces · Scenario dashboards

Relevant clients

For product manufacturers, developers, engineering consultancies, corporate sustainability teams, research programmes and public agencies that need a usable technical application—not just a report.

Typical work

  • Interactive product-selection and comparison tools for glazing, insulation, cool roofs, HVAC and other building technologies
  • Energy, carbon, savings, cost–benefit, payback and life-cycle-cost calculators
  • Custom simulation interfaces, automated parametric workflows and scenario-comparison dashboards
  • Development of calculation methods, assumptions, input datasets and validated performance models
  • Prototypes, user journeys, quality checks, documentation and coordination of software engineering and deployment
Illustrative brief · not a completed project

A building-products company wants its sales team to select a city, building type and combination of glazing, insulation and cool-roof products, then compare estimated energy and cooling impacts alongside indicative costs. The tool can use suitable physics-based simulations, precomputed scenarios or simplified models, with assumptions and limits clearly documented.

Typical outputs

Tool concept and requirements, interactive prototypes, calculation engines, product libraries, working web applications, test cases and handover guidance.

05Approach

Clarity in what matters.Rigour in the method.Imagination in the outcome.

Whether you need a simulation, an ESG baseline, a digital decision-support tool or a research study, we begin with the objective, available evidence, constraints and the outputs you need.

01 / Discuss

Understand the brief

Tell us what you are planning, deciding or trying to verify. We identify the main questions and constraints.

02 / Scope

Agree the method

We define the tasks, inputs, deliverables, programme and fees in a written scope of work.

03 / Investigate

Do the technical work

We measure, model, calculate, compare or develop and test a tool—using methods suited to the assignment.

04 / Deliver

Make findings useful

We provide a clear report, design guidance, working application, documentation or actionable roadmap, as agreed in the scope.

05 / Support

Take the next step

Where included in the engagement, we help interpret the results, respond to queries or review implementation.
